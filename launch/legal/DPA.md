# Data Processing Addendum

Last updated: [DATE]

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") is part of the HeyBell [Terms of Service](/terms) between you (the "Customer") and [ENTITY] ("HeyBell"). It applies whenever HeyBell processes personal data on your behalf while providing the service. You accept it by accepting the Terms; no signature is needed. If you need a signed copy, email [privacy@heybell.app].

## 1. Definitions

"**Data Protection Law**" means every law that applies to the processing of Customer Personal Data, including the EU GDPR, the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, the Swiss FADP, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended (CCPA) and other US state privacy laws, and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act) and its Rules, each as they apply.

"**Customer Personal Data**" means personal data that HeyBell processes for the Customer under the Terms, mainly the messages and identifiers of the people who chat with the Customer's agents ("**End Users**").

"**Controller**", "**processor**", "**data subject**", "**personal data breach**" and "**processing**" have the meanings in the GDPR. They include the equivalent terms in other Data Protection Law: "business" and "service provider" (CCPA), "controller" and "processor" (US state laws), and "Data Fiduciary", "Data Processor" and "Data Principal" (DPDP Act).

"**Subprocessor**" means a third party HeyBell engages to process Customer Personal Data.

## 2. Roles

1. For Customer Personal Data, the Customer is the controller (or business, or Data Fiduciary) and HeyBell is its processor (or service provider, or Data Processor).
2. HeyBell is a separate controller for Customer account, billing and website data, as described in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy). This DPA does not cover that data.
3. Each party will comply with the Data Protection Law that applies to it. The Customer is responsible for having a lawful basis and giving the notices and obtaining the consents needed for HeyBell to process Customer Personal Data, including consent to message End Users first (Terms section 3).

## 3. Instructions and permitted purposes

1. HeyBell will process Customer Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions. The Terms, this DPA and the Customer's configuration of its agents (including tools it connects and messages it schedules) are those instructions. HeyBell will tell the Customer promptly if it believes an instruction breaks Data Protection Law, and may pause that processing.
2. The specific business purposes (CCPA) for which HeyBell processes Customer Personal Data are:
   - receiving End User messages and routing them to the right agent;
   - generating and delivering agent replies and messages the Customer schedules;
   - remembering facts End Users share so the agent can help them in later conversations with the same Customer;
   - showing conversations, data and analytics to the Customer in the dashboard;
   - counting conversations for billing;
   - security, abuse prevention, debugging and keeping the service working;
   - complying with law.
3. HeyBell will **not**:
   - sell or share (as those terms are defined in the CCPA) Customer Personal Data;
   - retain, use or disclose it for any purpose other than the purposes above, or outside the direct business relationship with the Customer;
   - combine it with personal data from other customers or from HeyBell's own sources, except as Data Protection Law allows for the purposes above;
   - use it to train, fine-tune or evaluate AI models, or to improve agents for other customers;
   - share an End User's memories or messages between different customers' agents;
   - send marketing to End Users.
4. HeyBell may create aggregated, de-identified statistics (for example, average reply time across the service) that cannot reasonably be linked to any person or customer, and will not attempt to re-identify them.
5. HeyBell will notify the Customer if it can no longer meet its obligations under the CCPA or this DPA. The Customer may then take reasonable steps to stop and remediate unauthorized processing, including by suspending the affected agent.

## 5. Confidentiality and security

1. HeyBell ensures that everyone authorized to process Customer Personal Data is bound by confidentiality.
2. HeyBell maintains the technical and organizational measures in **Annex 1**, and may update them as long as overall protection does not decrease.

## 6. Subprocessors

1. The Customer gives general authorization for HeyBell to use the subprocessors listed at [/subprocessors](/subprocessors).
2. HeyBell will give at least **30 days' notice** of a new subprocessor by updating that page and emailing account owners who subscribe. The Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period. If we cannot address the objection, the Customer may end the affected service and receive a refund of prepaid fees for the remaining period.
3. HeyBell imposes data protection terms on each subprocessor that are at least as protective as this DPA, and remains responsible for its subprocessors' performance.
4. Messaging networks the Customer chooses (Apple iMessage, Telegram, WhatsApp) process messages under their own terms and are not HeyBell subprocessors for that processing.

## 7. Data subject requests

HeyBell will, taking into account the nature of the processing, help the Customer respond to requests from End Users to access, correct, delete, port, restrict or object to processing of their data, and to withdraw consent. The dashboard lets the Customer view and delete conversations. End Users can text STOP to stop messages and "delete my data" to erase their messages and memories with that agent. If HeyBell receives a request directly, it will refer the End User to the Customer, unless the request is an opt-out or deletion it can carry out itself, in which case it will carry it out and tell the Customer.

## 8. Personal data breaches

1. HeyBell will notify the Customer **without undue delay, and in any case within 48 hours**, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data. We aim for 24 hours.
2. The notice will describe, as far as known: what happened, the data and approximate number of people affected, likely consequences, steps taken and planned, and a contact. HeyBell will update the Customer as it learns more.
3. HeyBell will help the Customer meet its own notification duties, for example to a supervisory authority within 72 hours (GDPR) or to India's Data Protection Board and affected Data Principals (DPDP Act).
4. Notification is not an admission of fault.

## 9. Assistance, records and audits

1. HeyBell will provide reasonable help with the Customer's data protection impact assessments and consultations with regulators, including a written description of HeyBell's data flows, AI providers, retention and security on request.
2. HeyBell keeps a record of processing activities as a processor.
3. HeyBell will make available the information reasonably needed to show compliance with this DPA. Once every 12 months (or after a breach, or when a regulator requires it), the Customer may audit HeyBell's compliance by sending a written questionnaire, which HeyBell will answer within 30 days. Where the questionnaire is not enough, an on-site or remote audit may take place by an independent auditor bound by confidentiality, on 30 days' notice, during business hours, at the Customer's cost.

## 10. Deletion and return

1. Message content is deleted automatically **90 days** after each message. Memories are deleted when the End User asks, after 12 months without a conversation, or when the agent is deleted. The Customer can delete conversations and agents earlier at any time.
2. When the Terms end, the Customer may export its conversations for **30 days**. After that HeyBell deletes Customer Personal Data from live systems within 30 days, and from backups as they expire (within a further 30 days), unless law requires HeyBell to keep it.
3. HeyBell keeps a minimal record of opt-outs (a one-way hash of the End User's identifier and the date) for as long as needed to honour them, because deleting it would allow the End User to be messaged again.
4. HeyBell will confirm deletion in writing on request.

2. **EU/EEA:** for transfers of Customer Personal Data from the EEA to HeyBell in a country without an adequacy decision, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914) are incorporated by reference: Module Two (controller to processor) where the Customer is a controller, Module Three (processor to processor) where the Customer is a processor. Clause 7 (docking) applies; Clause 9 option 2 (general authorization, 30 days' notice); Clause 11 optional language does not apply; Clause 17 and 18: the law and courts of Ireland. Annex I is section 4 of this DPA, Annex II is Annex 1, Annex III is the subprocessor page.
3. **UK:** the UK International Data Transfer Addendum (version B1.0) to those clauses applies, with Table 4: either party may end it as allowed by its Section 19.
4. **Switzerland:** the clauses apply with references to the GDPR read as the FADP and the competent authority as the FDPIC.
5. HeyBell's onward transfers to subprocessors rely on their own Standard Contractual Clauses or EU-US Data Privacy Framework certification.

## 12. India (DPDP Act)

Where the DPDP Act applies, HeyBell acts as the Customer's Data Processor under a valid contract (this DPA) and will: implement the reasonable security safeguards in Annex 1, including logging and monitoring of access and keeping those logs for at least one year; delete Customer Personal Data when the Customer instructs or when the purpose is complete; and notify the Customer of breaches as in section 8 so the Customer can inform the Data Protection Board and affected Data Principals. Where HeyBell processes personal data of people outside India under a contract with a Customer outside India, the parties record that the processing is carried out under that contract.

## 13. Liability and order of precedence

Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limits in the Terms, except where Data Protection Law does not allow them. If this DPA conflicts with the Terms, this DPA wins for Customer Personal Data. If the Standard Contractual Clauses conflict with this DPA, the clauses win.

## Annex 1: Technical and organizational measures

**Encryption.** All traffic to HeyBell and between HeyBell and its subprocessors uses TLS. Data is encrypted at rest on our hosting and backup providers' storage. Credentials Customers give their agents (API keys, Telegram bot tokens) are additionally encrypted in our database with a key stored separately from the data.

**Separation.** Each Customer's agents, conversations and memories are stored against that Customer's agent and are never used by another Customer's agent. End User records are per agent: the same phone number messaging two businesses creates two unrelated records.

**Access control.** Production access is limited to named HeyBell staff who need it, with multi-factor authentication on hosting, database and code accounts. Customer dashboard sign in uses email magic links or Google, with sessions that expire.

**Logging and monitoring.** Service events, sign ins and administrative actions are logged without message content and kept for at least one year. Health checks and alerts watch the service around the clock; errors are reported without message text or phone numbers.

**Abuse limits.** Rate limits on sign up, sign in, agent builds and messages; spending caps on AI usage; a service-wide kill switch for inbound or outbound messaging.

**Backups and recovery.** Database snapshots every 30 seconds to encrypted storage, hourly copies for 48 hours and daily copies for 30 days; restores are tested.

**Data minimization.** Agents are instructed not to ask for payment card numbers, government IDs or health data. AI providers do not train on our data and delete inputs and outputs within 30 days.

**Opt-outs.** STOP and similar keywords are honoured on every channel and checked before every message the agent sends first.

**Deletion.** Automatic deletion of message content after 90 days; End User "delete my data" command; deletion of agents and accounts from the dashboard.

**Vendors.** Subprocessors are reviewed for security and bound by data protection terms (section 6).

**Incidents.** A written incident response plan covering containment, investigation, notification (section 8) and review.
