# Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This policy is part of the HeyBell [Terms of Service](/terms). It explains what you may and may not do with HeyBell agents. We wrote it to protect the people your agent talks to, the shared iMessage lines every HeyBell customer depends on, and the messaging platforms and AI providers we build on.

If you break this policy we may pause an agent, block a number or end your account, as described in section 8.

## 1. Who your agent may message

Your agent may only message people who asked to hear from you. In practice:

1. **People who message first.** Someone who texts your agent's code, scans your QR code, opens your Telegram bot or writes to your WhatsApp number has started the conversation. Your agent may reply to them. [R4, R7]
2. **People who opted in.** You may have your agent text someone first only if that person gave you their number and agreed to receive messages from your business on that channel, for example through the HeyBell share page with its consent checkbox, or your own checkout or signup form with clear consent wording. You must be able to show us that consent if we ask. [R4]
3. **You and your team.** You may send your agent to your own phone and to test phones you control.

You must not:

- upload, paste or type phone numbers or handles of people who have not opted in, including purchased, rented, scraped or shared lists;
- use "text me" invites, scheduled messages or the API to send cold outreach, sales prospecting or bulk announcements;
- send marketing messages (promotions, discounts, abandoned cart nudges) unless the person gave consent that covers marketing messages from you;
- message anyone between 9pm and 8am in their local time, unless they messaged you within the last 24 hours. [R4]

## 2. Stopping and help

Anyone can stop your agent at any time. HeyBell handles these keywords for you on every channel: **STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, OPTOUT, REVOKE** and **HELP, INFO**. A person who stops your agent will not be messaged again by it until they message it again themselves.

You must not:

- tell your agent to ignore, argue with or delay an opt out, or to ask people to stay;
- re-add someone who opted out, under a different number, channel or bot;
- make it harder to opt out than to opt in. [R4, R5]

## 3. Being honest about AI

Your agent is an AI. People have a right to know that.

- Do not instruct your agent to claim it is a human, to deny being an AI when asked, or to use a real employee's name and photo as if a person were typing.
- Keep HeyBell's first-message disclosure ("an AI assistant for <your business>") switched on. You may change the wording, but not remove the fact that it is an AI.
- When a person asks for a human, your agent must offer a way to reach one (handoff, email or phone), or say clearly when none is available. [R9]

## 4. What your agent may not do

Do not build or run an agent that:

1. **Breaks the law,** including consumer protection, telemarketing, privacy, anti-discrimination, export control and sanctions law.
2. **Deceives people,** for example fake reviews, fake scarcity, invented prices, made-up medical, financial or legal claims, phishing, or pretending to be another business or a government body.
3. **Handles high-risk decisions without a human.** Do not use an agent to make final decisions about someone's access to credit, insurance, housing, employment, education, healthcare or legal rights. Agents in healthcare, legal or financial settings must tell people they are AI and that a qualified professional should review the advice. [R11]
4. **Collects sensitive data it does not need.** Do not ask for full payment card numbers, bank passwords, government ID numbers, health records, biometric data, precise location (beyond a delivery address), or data about sex life, religion or political views. Take payments through a payment link from your payment provider, never in chat. [R6]
5. **Targets children.** HeyBell is not for agents aimed at people under 18, or under the age of digital consent where they live. [R6]
6. **Sells regulated or restricted goods** by chat where the law or the platform forbids it: weapons, ammunition, drugs (including prescription drugs and cannabis where not legal), tobacco and vaping products, alcohol without age checks, gambling, adult content and sexual services, payday loans, debt collection, crypto investment schemes, multi-level marketing recruitment. Telegram and WhatsApp commerce policies are stricter than ours in places and also apply. [R7, R8]
7. **Produces harmful content,** including harassment, hate, threats, sexual content involving minors, content that encourages self-harm, or instructions for weapons or serious crime. These are also banned by our AI providers' usage policies. [R11]
8. **Pretends to be a general-purpose AI assistant.** On WhatsApp, an agent must serve your business's customers about your business. General chat assistants are not allowed on the WhatsApp Business Platform. [R8]
9. **Attacks or overloads HeyBell** or anyone else: probing for vulnerabilities, bypassing limits or plan gates, sharing one plan across unrelated businesses, reselling access without a written agreement, or reverse engineering our shared lines.

## 5. Websites and content you give us

- Only point HeyBell at websites, documents and APIs that you own or are allowed to use for this purpose. Do not ask HeyBell to build an agent from a competitor's or anyone else's website. [R12]
- Our crawler identifies itself as HeyBellBot and follows robots.txt. Do not try to get around a site's robots.txt or login.
- Do not upload content that infringes someone else's copyright, trademark or privacy.

## 6. Shared iMessage lines

On Free, Starter and Growth plans, agents from many businesses share HeyBell's iMessage lines. If one agent spams, every business on that line can lose it. So on shared lines:

- your agent may open at most **[20]** new conversations a day by texting first (owner invites, share page sign ups and scheduled messages combined); replies to people who message first are not limited;
- links in your agent's messages must go to your own domain, your store or well-known services (payment, shipping, maps); no link shorteners;
- we may slow, queue or refuse first messages that look like bulk sending, and we may pause an agent whose messages get reported as junk.

Plans with a dedicated number get higher limits, but sections 1 to 5 still apply. [R1, R2]

## 7. Platform rules

You must also follow the rules of every channel your agent uses and of our AI providers. The main ones are:

- Apple: the iCloud and Apple Media Services terms that govern iMessage, including rules against spam;
- Telegram: the Telegram Terms of Service and Bot Developer Terms (your bot needs its own privacy policy, and HeyBell's end-user notice can serve as a starting point);
- WhatsApp: the WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy and Commerce Policy;
- Anthropic: the Anthropic Usage Policy, which applies to every reply your agent writes.

If a platform's rule is stricter than this policy, the platform's rule wins.

## 8. What happens if this policy is broken

We look at reports from people, platforms and our own monitoring. Depending on how serious and how repeated the problem is, we may:

1. warn you and ask you to fix it;
2. pause scheduled and first messages for the agent;
3. pause the agent, or remove it from a shared line;
4. suspend or end your account without refund, and report illegal activity to the authorities.

Where we can, we will tell you first and give you time to fix it. We will act first and tell you after when people are being harmed, a platform has warned us, or a shared line is at risk.

## 9. Reporting abuse

To report an agent that messaged you without permission or broke this policy, reply STOP to it and email **abuse@heybell.app** with the number or bot name and a screenshot. We reply within 2 business days.
